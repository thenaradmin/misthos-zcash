§ 01Keys

Each key lifts one line.

Each employee is paid from their own child wallet, so its viewing key reads that line and nothing else.

Checked on testnet. Alice's key sees her funding note and her payout, and nothing of Bob's.

A key is forever. It shows all of that employee's payouts, past and future; there is no revocation.

§ 02Blocks

Two steps, 11 and 19 blocks after funding.

The child wallet adds a hop, so each payout waits a random number of blocks after funding.

Step 1 · funding

One transaction from the treasury

Each employee's pay, plus 0.0001 ZEC for the payout fee, to their child wallet.

Delay

Random block waits

2 to 6 blocks per step by default, recorded before waiting so a restart never redraws them.

Step 2 · payout

Each child wallet pays its employee

One note in, one output, no change. A rerun skips anyone already paid.

Honest limit: on a quiet testnet, timing alone can still link funding to payouts. The delays shrink that signal; they don't remove it.

§ 03Proof

Show one line to a lender.

The employer signs every payout. A lender checks one employee's proof against the chain, not a screenshot.

  • The employer signs the network, the payout txid, the child wallet, the amount in zatoshis and the pay period.
  • The employer exports one proof for one employee: those fields, the signature and that child wallet's viewing key.
  • The lender runs verify-proof --lwd: signer, mined transaction and signed amount, checked against the chain.

On the recorded run, verify-proof --lwd accepted each employee's proof and rejected:

  • the other employee's txid
  • the funding txid
  • a changed amount
  • a changed period
  • a changed signature
  • a different employer key
§ 04Deductions

What the bars don't cover.

  • Testnet only. Mainnet addresses and a mainnet network setting are refused in code.
  • Timing. Funding and payouts can still be linked by when they land, as above.
  • The desk can't pay. Payouts and proofs run only from the CLI; the dashboard's pay and proof endpoints answer 410 Gone.
Six more deductions
  • Headcount shows. The funding transaction's public action count (3 for 2 employees) roughly reveals how many people were paid.
  • Every payout has the same shape. 1 note in, 1 output, 2 actions and a 10,000 zat fee, sent in roster order. That fixed shape helps link payouts to their funding transaction.
  • Keys can't be revoked. A shared employee key keeps reading that employee's payouts from this employer.
  • One extra transaction per employee per run, for the funding hop that makes per-employee proofs possible.
  • Lightwalletd learns a little. pay --two-step asks it directly for the funding txid, which tells its operator which IP cares about that txid.
  • Not built: NEAR Intents, the zakura-one-time-ivk crate, ZIP 311 payment disclosures (still a draft). Transactions are built by zingo-cli.

CLI only

The desk does not pay. A one-click payout straight from the treasury could not be proven per employee, so payouts run only from the CLI with pay --two-step.

Pay and prove

from a terminal
  1. Pay through per-employee child wallets, with random block delays between funding and payouts.
    payroll pay roster.csv --two-step --label oct-2026
  2. Export one employee's employer-signed proof. There is no download here: it had no login.
    payroll export-proof roster.csv --employee <id> --label oct-2026
  3. The employee's lender checks the signature and the chain.
    payroll verify-proof proof.txt <txid> --lwd https://<testnet lightwalletd>:443
